Grading guide
  • A — pristine: uniform colour, no wrinkling, no leakage, no mould, no visible marks.
  • B — sound: intact skin, natural ripening progression, no damage.
  • C — worn: intact but visibly bruised, scarred or discoloured; no wet leakage or mould.
  • D — deteriorated: extensive discolouration, dents or soft spots, heavy visible deterioration.
  • Spoilage: fresh → mild decline (early soft spots or dulling) → spoiled (clear rot/mould/leakage in places) → severely spoiled (majority of surface affected).
  • Ripeness: judge by skin colour only — green/unripe through fully coloured/soft-ripe, species-specific stages shown per item.
